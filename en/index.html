Frequently asked questions

Is there a set-up fee?

No. Building your website is included in the monthly subscription. Only optional services (visual identity, specific features) are billed separately, on quotation.

How long is the commitment?

You choose: 24 months (reference rate), 12 months (slightly higher monthly payment) or 24 months paid upfront with 2 months free. The commitment starts when your subscription is confirmed, then renews for 12-month periods, and you can opt out with two months' notice. Full details are in our terms of sale (in French).

How many changes can I request?

A monthly allowance of content changes is included. For bigger needs (new features), we send you a quote.

Do I own my content?

Yes. Your text, photos and logos remain your property. During the subscription we keep the website and domain name; if you have paid all amounts due by the end of the contract, they are transferred to you. Otherwise, we give you a copy of your content.

How do I pay?

Monthly payments are collected by SEPA direct debit or by bank card. Upfront payment can be made by SEPA direct debit, bank transfer or card. Invoices are sent electronically.

Can I have a mobile app?

Yes, where it makes sense, for example for a private area or intranet your team uses every day. The app is installed from the browser onto the phone's home screen, with your icon and no app store. Tell us what you need and we will say whether it suits your project.

Can you design my logo?

Yes, with a professional partner graphic designer. It is a separate service, quoted individually, that you can add to your subscription.
