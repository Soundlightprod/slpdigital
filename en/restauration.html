Questions from restaurant owners

I have no professional photos. Is that a problem?

No, you can start with your own photos. A partner graphic designer can also create your logo and visuals, as a quoted option.

Who updates the menu and opening hours?

We do. A number of updates is included each month, depending on your plan.

Can my customers book a table online?

With the Calendar & bookings plan, customers send a booking request that you confirm. You stay in charge of your dining room.

How long before I'm online?

You see a first mock-up within 7 working days if you supply your graphic assets, then the site goes live once you approve it.

Am I tied in?

The commitment is 24 months (reference rate) or 12 months, from confirmation of your subscription. Details are in the terms of sale (in French).
