Prices

Prices in euros, including 20% VAT. Options (visual identity, bespoke visuals) and specific features are quoted separately. Full details in the terms of sale (in French).

Commitment terms

  • 24-month (reference rate) or 12-month commitment, starting when your subscription is confirmed.
  • Automatic renewal for 12-month periods; you can opt out with two months' notice.
  • Mock-up within 7 working days if you supply your graphic assets; otherwise, no later than 30 days after the quote is approved if we create the brand guidelines.
  • Two rounds of corrections included; you approve everything before it goes live.
  • Payment by SEPA direct debit (bank card as a fallback); upfront payment by direct debit, transfer or card.
  • If you have paid all amounts due by the end of the contract, the website and domain name are transferred to you; otherwise, you receive a copy of your content.
